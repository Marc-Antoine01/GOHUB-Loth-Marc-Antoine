Source: `eazo.mp4` (1280×720 @ 30 fps, video.twimg.com, git-ignored). Analysis: scene detection, a 48-frame contact sheet,

## Camera ("same camera settings as the whatships video")
- **Locked-off, frontal, 2D.** No 3D orbits. The camera is a flat lens: slow push-ins (≈3–6 % per shot), gentle drifts, and a rare snap zoom into a word or a UI detail.
- **Almost no hard cuts:** one in 89 s (scene detection, threshold 0.3). Scenes change through **graphic wipes**: stacked horizontal colour bars slide across (the orange stripes), a colour field expands from a point, or an object scales up to fill the frame.
- Type and objects move, not the camera.

## Type
- **Word-by-word build:** each word rises from its baseline with a small blur-to-sharp and overshoot, about one word per beat ("A / parent / said …", "Drag / one / on.").
- One **accent word** per line, in the brand colour; the rest is near-black.
- Large display lines with tight tracking; small mono captions (a code-like comment line, e.g. `// …`) sit above or below as a texture.
- Blur as emphasis: a word starts blurred ("a vague idea") and sharpens.
